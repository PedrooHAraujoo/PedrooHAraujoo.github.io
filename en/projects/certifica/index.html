Context

A company that certifies lifting equipment typed every report by hand from the equipment's metal nameplate. The demo was built for one meeting: to show that AI reading works and, just as important, where it fails.

What I built

  • Nameplate reading from the phone camera, with structured output and per-field confidence.
  • Report generation in the client's own file (XLSX) plus two PDFs.
  • History with expiry dates and recertification from old spreadsheets.

Engineering decisions

  1. 01

    Respect the client's file

    The report is not recreated: the original template's XML is patched cell by cell, preserving the chart, images and styles the client already uses.

  2. 02

    Say where the AI fails

    On worn plates the model is wrong with high confidence. That is measured against an answer key with real plates and documented; human review therefore stays mandatory.

How it was built

Here AI is the product: Gemini vision with structured output, failover between two models when one is unavailable, and a 30-second limit per reading.
